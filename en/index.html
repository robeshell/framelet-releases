Framelet is a native Mac screenshot tool that lives in the menu bar. Your capture stays exactly where it was on screen; mark it up, press Return, and the annotated image is on your clipboard.

Requires macOS 15 or later. Available in English and Chinese.

01

One capture, start to finish

  1. 1

    Press the shortcut

    ⌃⇧4 captures an area. Framelet freezes the screen, so what you see is what you get.

  2. 2

    Select

    A loupe shows pixel coordinates and the color value, with the live size beside it. Release to confirm, nudge with the arrow keys, or press Space to capture a window.

  3. 3

    Annotate in place

    The toolbar appears next to the selection. Arrow A, Rectangle R, Text T, Counter N, Redact X — one letter per tool.

  4. 4

    Copy and done

    ↩ or ⌘C copies the annotated image, ⌘S saves, Esc leaves.

02

What it does

Annotate in place

17 tools. Select, Arrow, Rectangle, Text, Pen, Counter, Redact and Crop sit on the toolbar; Ellipse, Line, Highlighter, Pixelate, Blur, Spotlight, Magnifier, Measure and Color Picker are under More. Keys 1–9 pick tools in order; Signal Orange by default, three stroke weights scaled to the capture.

Recognize Text

⌃⇧O, select an area, and its text and any QR code contents go straight to the clipboard. While annotating you can open the text panel, edit, then copy. Recognition runs on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework.

Pin

Pin a capture at 1:1 in its original place as a reference. Scroll to zoom, ⌥-scroll for opacity, turn on click-through, double-click to close.

Quick Access and History

Captures can wait in the bottom-left corner (up to 5) and be dragged straight into other apps. ⌃⇧H opens History, grouped by day and searchable by title and by the text in each image.

Redact and Backdrop

Redactions are burned in when you copy or save, and the Magnifier can't sample the text underneath. Backdrop adds a background color, padding, rounded corners and a shadow in one step.

App Rules and automation

Set the after-capture action, format, scale, save location and annotation color per app. framelet://capture links work from Shortcuts — see URL automation.

Two experimental features — Scrolling Capture and screen recording (with MP4 / GIF export) — live under More Capture Modes in the menu bar, with settings in Settings › Experimental.

04

It stays on your Mac

  • Captures, annotations, recognized text and history are processed and stored on this Mac and never uploaded.
  • No account. No analytics, no usage data collected.
  • Screen Recording is used only for capturing. Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring are requested only when you turn on the experimental feature that needs them.
